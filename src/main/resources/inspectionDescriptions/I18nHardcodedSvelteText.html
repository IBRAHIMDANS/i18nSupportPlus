Reports a text written as is in the markup of a Svelte component.

Example: <button>Save</button>, <input placeholder="Name">.

Off by default. The quick fix extracts the text into a key and writes svelte-i18n's store, {$_('save')}, importing it when the component does not.

Needs the Svelte plugin. Runs in the editor and headless, in Qodana or inspect.sh.
