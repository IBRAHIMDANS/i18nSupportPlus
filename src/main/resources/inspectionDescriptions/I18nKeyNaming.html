Reports a key of a translation file that does not follow the project's naming convention: camelCase, snake_case, kebab-case, or a pattern of your own.

Example: user_name in a camelCase project. Each segment of a key is checked on its own; a plural form is checked without its suffix (itemCount_one passes in camelCase), and a segment made of digits only passes every convention.

Off by default: pick the convention in the options below. Rename to… opens the key rename with the converted name filled in: the key moves in every locale and at every call site.

Runs in the editor and headless, in Qodana or inspect.sh.
