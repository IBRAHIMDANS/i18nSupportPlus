Reports a text written as is in the HTML of a Blade view.

Example: <button>Save</button>, <input placeholder="Name">.

Off by default. The quick fix extracts the text into a key and writes Laravel's helper, {{ __('save') }}. A text an echo or a directive splits, Hello {{ $name }}!, is left alone.

Needs the Blade plugin. Runs in the editor and headless, in Qodana or inspect.sh.
