Reports a translation that does not follow the project's glossary for a term its reference holds.

Example: with Acme kept as written, Welcome to Acme becoming Bienvenue chez Akmé; with cart = fr: panier, Your cart becoming Votre chariot.

The glossary is the one of Settings | Tools | i18n Support Plus | Machine Translation, read even when machine translation is off. Terms are matched as whole words, ignoring case; inflected forms are not recognised. Off by default; without a glossary, nothing is reported.

Runs in the editor and headless, in Qodana or inspect.sh.
