Reports a text written as is in an Angular template.

Example: <button>Save</button>, <input placeholder="Name">.

Off by default. The quick fix extracts the text into a key and writes the pipe of the project's library: {{ 'save' | translate }} with ngx-translate, | transloco with Transloco. A tag carrying translate, transloco or Angular's i18n is left alone.

Runs in the editor and headless, in Qodana or inspect.sh.
