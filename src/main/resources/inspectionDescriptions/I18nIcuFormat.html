Reports an ICU plural or select lacking its other case, the only one ICU requires, or an unbalanced brace.

Example: {count, plural, one {# file}} has no other case: any count but one breaks the message. {count, plural, other {# files}} and {count, plural, =0 {no file} other {# files}} are complete.

Runs in the editor and headless, in Qodana or inspect.sh.
