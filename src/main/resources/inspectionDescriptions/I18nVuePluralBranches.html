Reports a vue-i18n plural value with fewer | branches than its locale's language has forms for an integer count.

Example: ru.json holding "{n} предмет | {n} предметов": Russian needs a branch for one, few and many, picked by the application's pluralRules.

Only missing branches are reported, never one too many: a zero form first is allowed. Read in a module whose preset is vue-i18n, or in a project without modules whose package.json depends on vue-i18n.

Runs in the editor and headless, in Qodana or inspect.sh.
