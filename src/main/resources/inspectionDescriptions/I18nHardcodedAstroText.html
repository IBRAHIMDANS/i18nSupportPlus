Reports a text written as is in the template of an Astro component.

Example: <button>Save</button>, <input placeholder="Name">.

Off by default. The quick fix extracts the text into a key and writes i18next's {t('save')}, importing t in the frontmatter when the component has none. {name} becomes a variable.

Needs the Astro plugin. Runs in the editor and headless, in Qodana or inspect.sh.
